Support

Write to support@8086.llc. Say which version of macOS you use and what you clicked; a person reads every message. Please do not send company data, API keys or your full licence key (the last four characters are enough).

First questions

What runs it?
SoloINC for Mac, a free download for macOS 26 or later from this site. The download opens soon. iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows and the Mac App Store are coming later.
Is it a chat assistant?
No. It runs a company of agents and brings you the decisions at the Desk. You can ask the Managing Agent once you add a model, but the Desk is the product.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no account with us and no server of ours. The company runs on your Mac.
What is the sample company?
Harbor & Pine, a made-up design studio with three decisions waiting, so you can try the Desk before setting anything up. Nothing in it is real, and it needs no model, key or network.
Which model does it use?
The one you bring. Your Claude subscription through Anthropic's Claude Code program (in Terminal, claude setup-token gives the token to paste), or an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI and other providers, or a model server on your own machine such as Ollama. No model ships inside the app, and nothing is sent until you add one.
What is free?
The sample company, the Desk, answering, the record, Stop everything, Today and export, with as many companies and routines of your own as you like. Your agents get 30 runs a month (60 in your first month); each step an agent works counts as one run, and the count starts again on the 1st. The Desk, answering, the record and Stop are never limited. Free never expires.
What does SoloINC Pro add?
No limit on agent runs. It is $12 a month or $99 a year (U.S. prices, before any tax), sold by Lemon Squeezy. Subscriptions open soon; until then the app's Subscription section says so.
How do I activate my licence key?
Lemon Squeezy emails a licence key with your subscription. In the app, open This install's card › Subscription, paste the key and press Activate. Pro then keeps working offline for 14 days between checks.
How do I move Pro to another Mac?
On the old Mac, This install's card › Subscription › Deactivate this Mac, then activate the key on the new one.
How do I cancel, or get a refund?
Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record and handles billing: manage or cancel the subscription through Lemon Squeezy (its receipt email says how), and write to us if you need help with a refund. What you made stays on your Mac whatever happens to the subscription.
How do I stop everything at once?
Press HALT at the top of the window and confirm "Stop everything". Starting again undoes it. Both are written to the record.
How do I erase everything?
Reset › Everything, then type the phrase it shows. See Deleting your data.
What do the legal routines give me?
AI analysis to take to your lawyer; they do not replace one.
Who is it for?
People 13 and over, mainly founders of one-to-five-person companies.
