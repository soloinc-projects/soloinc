Support

Write to support@8086.llc. Say which device you use and what you tapped; a person reads every message. Please do not send company data or keys.

First questions

Is it a chat assistant?
No. It runs a company of agents and brings you the decisions at the Desk. You can talk to the Managing Agent once you add a model, but the Desk is the product.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no account and no sign-in. The company runs on your own device.
What is the sample company?
Harbor & Pine, a made-up company with three decisions waiting, so you can try the Desk before setting anything up. Nothing in it is real. Remove it from the banner or from Settings.
Does it work without a network?
Opening the Desk, answering, reading the record and stopping work with the network off. An answer given offline is shown as "waiting to apply" and is applied when it can be; if something changed meanwhile, you see both and choose. Agents that use an online model or a connector need the network for that step.
Which model does it use?
The one you choose: your own key from a provider. No model ships inside the app. The sample company needs none. Nothing is sent until you add one.
What does the unlock add?
Every routine in the library for a company of your own (a company runs five free), and more than one company. It is $29.99 in the U.S., paid once, through your device's store. The Desk, answering, the record, Stop, Today and export are free.
How do I restore my purchase?
Settings › Unlock SoloINC › Restore purchase, signed in to the same store account. Erasing the app's data keeps your purchase.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple or Google Play.
How do I pair my phone with my computer?
In the SoloINC console on the computer: Settings › Devices › Pair a phone shows a code. On the phone: Settings › Pair with a Mac or PC, then scan the code. The two talk over your own network. Pairing is free.
How do I stop everything at once?
Tap Stop on the Desk, then "Stop everything". Start again undoes it. Both are written to the record.
How do I erase everything?
Settings › Erase everything on this device, then type ERASE. See Deleting your data.
Who is it for?
People 13 and over, mainly founders of one-to-five-person companies. This app is SoloINC on your device; the web console is a separate product.
