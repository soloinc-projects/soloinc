AI agents run the work. You decide at the Desk.

SoloINC runs a company of AI agents on your own phone, tablet or computer. Every decision that needs a person waits for you as a memo with its numbers, and every act leaves a line you can read back.

Free to download. One unlock, $29.99 in the U.S., paid once, adds pairing with the SoloINC console on a Mac or PC and more than one company. Privacy in one line: nothing leaves your device unless you open a door.

One Desk for every decision

Each memo shows the question, the numbers, the files and what your answer will do. Answer it in one tap.

A line for every act

The record keeps one line for every act, by you or an agent, with the value before and after. Undo it, or export it all as one file.

On your device, no account

The company runs where you are. No account, nothing in our cloud, no analytics, no ads. Stop everything in two taps.

Who it is not for

  • It is not a chat assistant: it runs a company and brings you the decisions.
  • There is no account and nothing in our cloud; the company runs on your own device. Teams that need SSO or fleet administration are not served yet.
  • For people 13 and over.
  • This app is SoloINC on your device. The web console is a separate product.
