AI agents run the work. You decide at the Desk.

SoloINC runs a company of AI agents on your Mac, with the model you bring. Every decision that needs a person waits for you at the Desk with its numbers, and every act leaves a line you can read back.

A free download for macOS 26 or later, signed and notarised by Apple, from this site (not the Mac App Store). iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows and the Mac App Store are coming later.

One Desk for every decision

Each decision shows the question, the numbers, the files and what your answer will do. Answer it, and the work goes on.

A line for every act

The record keeps one line for every act, by you or an agent, with the value before and after. Undo it, or export a period as one file.

Your Mac, your model

The company runs on your Mac. No account with us and no server of ours: bring your Claude subscription or an API key. Stop everything with one button.

Free, and SoloINC Pro

Free, never gated

The sample company, the Desk, answering, the record, Stop everything, Today and export, with as many companies and routines of your own as you like. Your agents get 30 runs a month (60 in your first month); each step an agent works counts as one run, and the count starts again on the 1st. The Desk, answering, the record and Stop are never limited. Free does not expire.

SoloINC Pro

No limit on agent runs. $12 a month or $99 a year (U.S. prices, before any tax), sold by Lemon Squeezy. Subscriptions open soon.

Bring your own model

Your Claude subscription through Claude Code, or an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI and other providers, or a model server on your own machine. Nothing is sent until you add one.

Who it is not for

  • It is not a chat assistant: it runs a company and brings you the decisions.
  • It does not include a model. Agents need your own Claude subscription, an API key or a local model server; the sample company needs none.
  • Its legal routines give AI analysis to take to your lawyer; they do not replace one.
  • Teams that need shared sign-in (SSO) or fleet administration are not served yet.
  • For people 13 and over.
