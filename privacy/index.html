Privacy policy

For the SoloINC apps on iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and Windows, published by 8086 LLC. Effective 26 October 2026.

In one line

Nothing leaves your device unless you open a door. We run no server for the app, and we receive none of your data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account and no sign-in, so it never asks for your name, email, phone number or password. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash-reporting service and no advertising identifier. It does not read your location, contacts, photos, calendar or health data.

What the app keeps on your device

  • Your companies: their teams, routines, jobs, documents, decisions and the record (one line for every act, with the value before and after).
  • The keys you add for a model provider or a connector, in the app's own private storage on the device. The device key that signs each of your answers is kept in the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore (on Windows, protected for your Windows user).
  • A search index of your company's content, so you can find a decision from the system's search (Spotlight on iPhone, iPad and Mac; App Search on Android, which only SoloINC itself reads).
  • The app's own event log (for example "a decision was answered", "a routine was stopped"), kept in monthly files on the device for 30 days; the app deletes older lines when it starts. Records of each model call stay on the device. Those records and the record itself are kept until you erase them.
  • Reports of the app's own crashes, written on the device when it crashes and kept until you erase them. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Your purchase of the unlock, mirrored on the device so the app works offline.
  • If you pair with the SoloINC console on a Mac or PC, a key for that pairing.

None of this is sent to us. It leaves the device only through a door below, or when you export it yourself.

Doors you can open

By default there are none. Each sends data only where you choose, to an account you hold:

  • A model. If you add your own key, the text the agents and the Managing Agent need is sent to that provider, under your own agreement with them.
  • Connectors. A connector you add sends what its job needs to that service, with your own credentials.
  • Pairing. A paired phone and desktop talk over your own network.
  • Export. You can export the last 30 days of decisions, record and documents as one file and share it where you choose.

Purchases

The one unlock is bought through the App Store or Google Play. The store handles the payment; the app never sees your card and receives only whether the purchase is yours.

Your age

The app asks the store for your age range each time it starts, so it can follow the law where one applies. It uses the answer in that session and does not store it. SoloINC is for people 13 and over and is not directed to children.

Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint

Before an answer is sent, the system checks you (Face ID, Touch ID or the passcode; the fingerprint or the screen lock). The app receives only pass or fail, never a fingerprint or a face.

Deleting your data

Everything is on your device, so you delete it there. In the app: Settings › Erase everything on this device. This removes: every company, its jobs, documents and record; the keys you added; the search index; the event log. This stays: your purchase. You can restore it. Deleting the app removes it all as well. There is no account to close and nothing held by us to delete. Data you sent through a door is held by the service you sent it to; delete it there.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visits as its own privacy statement describes.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with its date. Questions: support@8086.llc. 8086 LLC publishes the app.
