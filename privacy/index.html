Privacy policy

For SoloINC for Mac, downloaded from this site, published by 8086 LLC. Effective 30 October 2026.

In one line

Nothing leaves your Mac at first launch. Later, data leaves only for a service you set up yourself, or for Lemon Squeezy when you use a SoloINC Pro licence key. We run no server for the app and receive none of your work.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account with us and no sign-in to us, so the app never sends us your name, email or password. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash-reporting service.

What the app keeps on your Mac

Everything below is kept in the folder .soloinc in your home folder.

  • Your companies: their teams, routines, jobs, documents, decisions and the record (one line for every act, with the value before and after).
  • The keys and tokens you add for a model provider or a connector.
  • The app's own log of how it is used (which screens and steps ran into faults or dead ends, with no field values or titles), kept in monthly files on your Mac. It is never sent anywhere.
  • If you activate SoloINC Pro: your licence key and the result of its last check, so Pro keeps working offline. The record and the screens show only the key's last four characters.

None of this is sent to us.

What leaves your Mac, and when

  • At first launch: nothing. Opening the app, the sample company and the main screens makes no network connection.
  • Your model. When you add your Claude subscription (through Anthropic's Claude Code program) or an API key from a provider such as Anthropic or OpenAI, the text the agents need for their work is sent to that provider, under your own agreement with them. A model server on your own machine keeps it on your machine. The app also reads the list of models from the providers you added.
  • Connectors. A connector you set up sends what its job needs to that service, with your own credentials.
  • Lemon Squeezy, for SoloINC Pro. When you press Activate, Deactivate this Mac or Validate now, the app sends your licence key and an instance name ("SoloINC for Mac") or the activation's id to Lemon Squeezy's licence service. While a key is active, the app checks it with Lemon Squeezy at most once a day. Nothing else of yours goes with it.
  • Public reads you ask for. If you press "Read the model list now" or "Look for an update", the app reads public model lists and SoloINC's release feed at soloinc.ai, and from then on reads them again up to four times a day. These requests carry the app's name and version and nothing of yours.

Payments

SoloINC Pro is sold by Lemon Squeezy, the merchant of record. You pay on Lemon Squeezy's own checkout; Lemon Squeezy handles your payment details and billing address under its privacy policy. We never see your card details. As the seller's platform, Lemon Squeezy tells us about orders (for example the email a licence key was sent to), which we use only to support your subscription.

Deleting your data

Everything is on your Mac, so you delete it there. In the app, Install › reset, with What to clear set to Everything, moves every company, key, licence key and backup into a restore point and starts the app again from its first run; forget that restore point to delete it for good. The record of what was done is kept by a reset. To remove every trace, quit SoloINC and delete the app and the .soloinc folder in your home folder. There is no account to close and nothing held by us to delete. Data you sent to a model provider or a connector is held by that service; delete it there. To deactivate a licence key before you delete, use Deactivate this Mac.

Children

SoloINC is for people 13 and over and is not directed to children.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visits as its own privacy statement describes.

Changes and contact
